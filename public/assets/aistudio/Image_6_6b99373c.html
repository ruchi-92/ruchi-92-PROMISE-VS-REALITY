What to check next

Practical, realistic verification steps for everyday consumers before escalating.

Consumer Protection Guide
1

Check for separate credit note or voucher ledger

Providers occasionally credit promotional offsets in a separate prepaid wallet or subsequent statement cycle. Log in to your provider app and check the "Credits & Discounts" tab.

2

Review Auto-Pay Enrollment status

Some ₹200 promotional credits require active bank e-mandate or credit card auto-pay to stay unlocked. Verify that your payment method did not expire or decline recently.

3

Look for mid-cycle plan upgrades or addons

Check if any speed boost trial, add-on OTT channel pack, or router rental upgrade was activated during this billing window, which may temporarily nullify promotional clauses.

4

Use our drafted clarification request

Send a polite, fact-based inquiry referencing your initial agreement date (Aug 14, 2025) and missing promotional line item. Click the draft button above to preview the structured letter.
