# PROMISE vs REALITY

### An AI-Powered Consumer Commitment Verification System

## About the Project

PROMISE vs REALITY is a project designed to help consumers check whether the actual outcome of a purchase or service matches the promises made by a seller or service provider.

Important information can be scattered across advertisements, offers, order confirmations, invoices, receipts, and contracts. Manually comparing these documents can be difficult.

Our goal is to make this process easier by extracting relevant information, comparing commitments with actual outcomes, and highlighting potential discrepancies.

## Problem Statement

Consumers may struggle to identify unexpected charges, missing discounts, incorrect quantities, or differences between advertised offers and the final transaction.

When information is spread across multiple documents, identifying these discrepancies becomes time-consuming.

## Proposed Solution

PROMISE vs REALITY aims to:

* Accept transaction-related documents and evidence.
* Extract important prices, discounts, quantities, fees, and conditions.
* Identify the commitments made before a transaction.
* Compare those commitments with the actual outcome.
* Highlight potential discrepancies with supporting evidence.
* Explain uncertainty when information is missing or unclear.

## How It Works

1. Upload relevant documents, such as an offer, order confirmation, invoice, or receipt.
2. Extract important information from the documents.
3. Identify the original commitments and actual transaction details.
4. Compare the extracted information.
5. Display potential discrepancies and supporting evidence.
6. Review the results.

## Limitations

Results depend on the quality and completeness of the supplied evidence and the accuracy of the extraction and comparison methods.

The system is intended to support consumer decision-making. Its findings should not automatically be treated as proof of fraud or legal wrongdoing.
